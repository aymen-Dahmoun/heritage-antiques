# Image credits

The catalogue images in `src/assets/images/` are curated public-domain and open-license photographs of museum-grade artifacts, historical instruments, and antiquities.

Catalogue listings, prices, and provenance texts are fictional and created for the Heritage & Co. demonstration. Strict curation standards are observed: no alcohol, wine, or barware items are present in this collection.
